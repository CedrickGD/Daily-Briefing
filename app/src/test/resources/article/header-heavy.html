Iranian leaders hailed the Democratic landslide in the U.S. midterms on Tuesday as a rejection of President Donald Trump’s war against Iran.

“The Iranian nation handed Trump a strategic defeat on the battlefield,” the head of the Iranian Supreme National Security Council said on Wednesday in a statement.

Democrats will take control of both chambers of Congress in January, giving them new leverage to challenge the war effort. Some Republicans now openly question it as well.

“The reason we lost is the war,” the Vice President posted on Wednesday, in an unusually blunt assessment of the results for his own party.

While some took credit for Trump’s defeat, other Iranian leaders cast the midterm results as a repudiation of his war by Americans who are tired of rising prices.

The sweeping Republican defeat has sharpened divisions over the war, and some lawmakers reportedly want to call the defense secretary to account for it.
